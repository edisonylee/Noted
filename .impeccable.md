# noted — design context

## Design Context

### Users
A single person (the maker) logging their own life — gym, food, mood, schedule — by typing,
snapping a photo of a handwritten note, or speaking. They open noted in quick moments (post-workout,
end of day) to dump a note, glance at their timeline/trends, or ask a quick question. It is a calm,
private, daily tool — a personal knowledge base of the self, not a team product.

### Brand Personality
Calm · refined · effortless. The interface should feel like a quiet, premium journal that happens
to be intelligent — it gets out of the way. Emotionally: confidence and ease, never busy or noisy.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **References:** ElevenLabs, Instagram — high-modern minimalism. **Anti-reference:** the current
  "dashboard / bright-blue card grid" look (reads dated and templated); also avoid AI-slop
  (cyan-on-dark, purple→blue gradients, neon, glassmorphism, emoji-as-icons).
- **Theme:** light, airy, **warm off-white** canvas (never pure white/black — everything tinted
  warm). Generous whitespace; hairline (1px) warm-gray borders instead of heavy shadows.
- **Color:** near-monochrome. Warm off-white surfaces, warm near-black **ink** for text and
  primary actions, warm stone grays for secondary/tertiary. **One restrained accent (warm clay)**
  reserved for identity + active/live states only (the chat assistant, active nav, focus) — not
  sprinkled around. Functional status colors (save/error) muted, used only to signal state.
- **Type:** a single clean grotesk (**Geist**) with strong weight/size contrast for hierarchy —
  NOT Inter/system defaults, no monospace-as-decoration.
- **Icons:** real icon set (**lucide-react**), hairline weight. **No emoji anywhere.**
- **Motion:** subtle and quick — opacity/transform only, ease-out; one tasteful entrance per view,
  no bounce.

### Design Principles
1. **Quiet by default, expressive on demand.** Chrome recedes; the user's content (notes, answers,
   charts) is the only thing with presence. The AI assistant is a floating presence you summon, not
   a panel cluttering the home view.
2. **Monochrome system, one accent with meaning.** Color is information, not decoration — the clay
   accent marks "the assistant / live / active," nothing else.
3. **Hairlines over boxes.** Separate with 1px warm lines and whitespace; avoid card-in-card and
   uniform drop-shadow grids. Not everything needs a container.
4. **Typographic hierarchy does the work.** Weight, size, and spacing — not color or borders —
   create structure.
5. **Effortless motion.** Smooth, fast, restrained; transitions explain state changes (expand /
   collapse / send), never call attention to themselves.

### Tokens (warm, near-monochrome)
- canvas `#F7F5F1` · surface `#FFFFFF`/`#FCFBF8` · ink `#1B1916` · ink-soft `#46413A` ·
  muted `#8C857A` · line `#E9E5DD` · accent (clay) `#BC5B3C` · success `#3F7D5B` · error `#BE463B`
- font: Geist · radii: 10–16px · borders: 1px `line` · motion: 160–240ms ease-out
